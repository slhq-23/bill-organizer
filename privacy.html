Privacy Policy

Bill Organizer: Paycheck Plan · Last updated September 29, 2026

Your bills and paychecks stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There is no account and no bank link, and we can't see your bills.

What stays with you

  • Your bills, paychecks, and payments are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is on for your Apple Account, the app also keeps a copy in your private iCloud storage so it syncs to your other devices. Only your Apple Account can read that copy. We can't.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They never pass through a server.
  • The widget and Siri shortcuts read your bills on your iPhone.

What we collect

To learn which features people use, the app sends a small set of anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider. For example: the app was opened, a paycheck was added, a bill was marked paid, the paywall was shown. Each event can include:

  • the event name, and for new bills a rough count range ("2–5 bills")
  • a random ID created when you install the app, which isn't tied to your name, email, or Apple Account
  • the device model, iOS version, app version, language, and time zone

The events never include bill names, amounts, dates, paycheck amounts, or anything you type. Like any internet request, they carry your network (IP) address; we don't use it to identify you.

We don't use advertising, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data.

Feedback you send

If you use Send feedback in the app, we receive what you type, the kind of feedback (idea, bug, or question), the app version, and whether you've unlocked the app. An email address is sent only if you enter one, and we use it only to reply. A random ID, separate from the analytics ID, limits how many messages one device can send. Feedback never includes your bills. It's stored on Cloudflare, our hosting provider, and read only by us.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment. We receive no payment details, only whether the unlock was purchased.

No account, no bank link

You never create an account, and the app never connects to your bank. It only knows the bills and paychecks you enter.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a bill or paycheck in the app to remove it from your iPhone and your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy as well, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • The usage events aren't linked to your name, email, or Apple Account, so we can't single out yours.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll say so in the app before the change takes effect.

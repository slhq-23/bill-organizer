How is "left before your next paycheck" worked out?

The app takes the paycheck that started your current pay period, and subtracts every bill due before your next paycheck, paid or not. A bill due on a payday belongs to the new paycheck. Unpaid bills from the last 30 days stay on the screen as Overdue and count too.

What does the red "short" number mean?

The bills due before your next paycheck add up to more than the paycheck that has to cover them. The note under the number names the biggest bill and when your next paycheck lands.

I don't get paid on a schedule. Can I still use it?

Yes. Skip the paycheck step, and the home screen lists the bills due in the next 30 days with a total. You can add a paycheck later.

My reminders don't arrive

  • Check that notifications are on: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Bill Organizer.
  • Check the reminder time in the app: Settings → Reminder time.
  • Reminders are for the next two months. Opening the app schedules the next ones.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, go to Settings → Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Family Sharing members get the unlock too.

Will my bills move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if iCloud is on for your Apple Account on both phones. The app syncs through your private iCloud storage. There's no account to sign in to.

How do I delete my data?

Delete bills and paychecks in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. See the privacy policy for details.
